What is Healthcare Heroes?

One email. One message worth sharing.

Healthcare Heroes is a peer-to-peer health movement in Weymouth and Portland. Each month, local people get one simple message about health and wellbeing, and pass it on to someone they care about.

People are far more likely to try something new when someone they trust mentions it. One honest conversation can do what a poster can't.

This month we're looking at

Living with cancer, and the people around it.

Cancer doesn't only affect the person with the diagnosis. Partners, children, friends and carers carry a lot too, often quietly. And the months after a diagnosis can feel lonely, even when people are trying to help.

Since September, a Cancer Support Group has been meeting in Weymouth. It's for anyone affected by cancer: people living with it, people who have finished treatment, and the people who love and care for them.

Cancer Support Group

9Mon · November

10am to 12pm at The Dry Dock, 6-7 St Thomas Street, Weymouth, DT4 8EW.
Drop in any time in that window. No booking, no need to stay the whole two hours, and no need to have been before. Coffee, company, and sharing only if you want to.
It meets on the second Monday of every month. See dates up to March.

The group is supported by Jo Gowing, Cancer Care Coordinator for Weymouth and Portland. Jo's role is non-clinical. She helps people with the practical and emotional side of cancer: what happens next, what support is out there, and feeling less alone with it. You don't need a referral to contact her.

When to contact your GP or get urgent help

  • New or unexplained symptoms you are worried about. Don't wait.
  • Symptoms getting worse during or after treatment
  • Side effects affecting daily life
  • If you are struggling emotionally, call NHS 111

Call 999 or go to A&E if someone is in immediate danger.
